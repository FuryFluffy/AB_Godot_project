# Weapon Arts MP Rebalance — 2026-08-03

The provisional two-Action techniques were replaced by MP-powered weapon arts.
All three cost 1 Action and 2 MP and remain Refuge unlocks with temporary
developer grants enabled.

- Forced Blade: Sword attack, +1 flat damage, and one additional Dodge success
  ignored on top of the equipped Sword rank.
- Knife Dance: separately rolled Dagger attacks against up to two distinct
  legal targets. Each target reacts independently. If only one target is legal,
  it is attacked once. The cost is paid once, and all follow-ups from the first
  attack finish before the second begins.
- Jaw Break: Staff attack with +1 flat damage. A successful hit applies Stunned
  only when the target used no Defense. A failed Dodge, Armor, Shield, or Parry
  attempt still counts as defending and prevents this Stun condition.

Flat damage is applied after defense only when at least one Attack success
remains. It then follows ordinary armor and shield damage allocation.

Weapon-family rank properties and unique weapon effects still layer onto each
art. No heroine stats, enemy stats, spells, basic attacks, counterattack range,
or Layer 1 balance values changed in this checkpoint.
